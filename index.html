Glimpses. 4013 / V • Entry 2 You were the most amazing person I had ever known. And I don’t think you will ever understand what you became to me. I never loved someone like this. I never looked at someone the way I looked at you. I never felt this fucking much for anyone. I went mad in love with you. That’s the truth. I was too much. Too attached. Too afraid. Too emotional. Too fucking in love. And somewhere in all of that, I lost myself. I started measuring my days by you. Your replies. Your silence. Your mood. The way you talked to me. The way you didn’t. I started noticing every little change. A different tone. A shorter reply. A little more distance. And my stupid mind would turn every small thing into a thousand different possibilities. Did I do something? Are you losing interest? Is there someone else? Do you still love me the same way? Am I still important to you? I hated the person I was becoming. But I couldn’t stop. Because I was scared of losing you. I had given you so much of me that the thought of you leaving felt like someone taking away a part of me that I didn’t know how to live without. And I expected you to hold me through it. That’s where I was wrong. I expected you to understand my insecurities. I expected you to calm the storms you didn’t create. I expected you to keep choosing me when I was constantly afraid you wouldn’t. And when you couldn’t… I broke. I overthought. I questioned. I reacted. I became someone even I didn’t recognize. I know that. I accept it. I know I hurt you too. But here’s the part I still struggle to admit— I didn’t become this insecure for no reason. Some of your actions hurt me. Some things you did made me question where I stood in your life. Some silences made me feel unwanted. Some changes in you made me feel like I was slowly losing you while you were still standing right in front of me. And maybe you never meant to make me feel that way. Maybe it was never your intention. But intention doesn’t erase what something made me feel. I was already scared of losing you. And sometimes, without even realizing it, you gave that fear a reason to breathe. Still… I don’t want to make you the villain. Because I wasn’t innocent either. I loved you badly sometimes. I held on too tightly. I asked for too much because I was terrified that one day I’d wake up and realize that I had become nothing to you. And maybe that’s exactly what happened. Maybe I loved you so much that I slowly turned love into fear. And fear into questions. And questions into fights. And fights into distance. Until one day… there was nothing left to fight for. That’s what destroys me. Not that I loved you. Not even that I lost you. It’s that somewhere between loving you with everything I had and being terrified of losing you… I lost the version of myself you fell in love with. And I don’t know if I’ll ever forgive myself for that. Because all I ever wanted was to love you. I just didn’t know how to love you without being afraid of losing you.
